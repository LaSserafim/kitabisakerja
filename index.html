National Employment Regulatory Compliance

In adherence to Indonesian Manpower Law No. 13/2003 and modern corporate ethics standards:

1. Strict Zero-Placement Fee

Employers on KitabisaKerja are legally bound not to charge administrative, testing, or processing fees of any kind.

2. Verified Corporate Entities

All job postings require formal validation of business registration (NIB), legitimate tax ID (NPWP), and verified corporate domains.

3. Structured Candidate Privacy

Personal data is strictly protected under Law No. 27/2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP) and never monetized to third parties.
